description: >-
 Final project for AMCS 312 at KAUST (High Performance Computing):
 A numerical sttudy of the circular hydraulic jump using the shallow water equations with a 4th-order viscosity term.
 The equations are discretized on a polar grid with a semi-implicit hybrid finite volume-finite difference scheme.
 We validate the solver on radially symmmetric tests against one-dimensional highly accurate solutions.
 The vanishing viscosity limit is studied for a circular hydraulic jump.
 Scalability tests are performed up to 1024 cores on the Ibex cluster at KAUST.
